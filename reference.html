The brief is the same tactile party-game vibe, using the existing RiffRaff cast, artwork and rules.

Reference found on the official controller site.

The installed-game catalogue explicitly excludes jackbox.tv controller assets. The public jackbox.tv manifest identifies the Party Pack 7 Quiplash 3 controller as version 5.824.421. Its published styles and screen templates were inspected as reference. This is source inspection, not an end-to-end capture of a live Jackbox room.

Deliberate RiffRaff differences

The game has three regular rounds and a shared Royale finale. The finale is not Quiplash 3’s three-response Thriplash. It uses one answer and three voting tokens. Two-human sessions add Clank. The prototype also proposes a six-letter manual room code, draft recovery, per-answer receipts, and local text/motion controls. These are labeled design choices, not claims about Quiplash features.

Artwork and typography

The existing logo, eight foam character front previews, and blue/tangerine clay background are reused from the local project. Originals are preserved. The QR frame is a new generated clay/foam study; its actual QR modules are deterministic SVG. Ziggy has no visual body. Rokkitt comes from the independently available Google Fonts distribution under the bundled SIL Open Font License. It is a prototype type choice, not a replacement for the planned Offcut lettering.
